# LinkedIn Outreach Automation

Scale your LinkedIn prospecting without losing the personal touch. Our tool lets you log in securely via LinkedIn, then build and run outreach campaigns end-to-end, with no manual sending required.

**What it does:**

- **Campaign builder:** Create campaigns, add prospects, and define your messaging strategy in one place.
- **AI-optimized messaging:** Get AI-driven recommendations to refine your outreach copy and structure your follow-up cadence, including how many messages to send and how many days apart, based on what tends to convert.
- **Automated sending:** The tool sends connection requests and scheduled follow-ups on your behalf, so your sequences run on autopilot once set up.
- **Campaign analytics dashboard:** Track acceptance rates, reply rates, and the live status of every lead in your pipeline, all from a single dashboard.

**Why it matters:**

Manually managing LinkedIn outreach means writing messages, tracking who accepted, and remembering when to follow up, none of which scales well. Our tool handles the repetitive work and uses AI to continuously sharpen your approach, so your team can focus on conversations that actually convert.
