# Myntmore Website – Ideal User Journey

## Objective

Guide visitors from their **first interaction with Myntmore** to **booking a strategy call**.

The website should be structured to move users through the following stages:

**Awareness → Understanding → Trust → Interest → Action**

Primary conversion goal:

**Book a Strategy Call**

---

# 1. Entry Points (Traffic Sources)

Visitors may arrive on the website from the following sources:

- LinkedIn Inbound
- WhatsApp Groups (Ascent, founder groups etc.)
- WeWork community
- Referrals (friends, clients, employees, family)
- Cold Emailing
- Social Media
- Performance Marketing
- Workshops / Networking Events / Webinars
- Cold Calling

| Home Page | Landing Page |
| --- | --- |
| Referrals (friends, clients, employees, family) | LinkedIn Cold Outreach |
| WeWork community | WhatsApp Groups (Ascent, founder groups etc.) |
| Social Media | Cold Emailing |
| Cold Emailing | Performance Marketing |
| Workshops / Networking Events / Webinars | Cold Calling |
| LinkedIn Inbound |  |

These visitors may have **different levels of familiarity with Myntmore**, so the website must quickly communicate the value proposition.

# 2. Landing Section (Hero Section)

**Purpose:**

Clearly communicate what Myntmore does and who it helps.

**Key elements:**

Headline

**Primary CTA**

**Secondary CTA**

---

# 3. Credibility Section

**Purpose:**

Establish trust early in the user journey.

**Elements:**

- Client logos
- Testimonials
- Key metrics or results
- Social proof

---

# 4. Problem Context Section

**Purpose:**

Highlight common lead generation challenges faced by the target audience.

Example themes:

- Difficulty generating consistent sales meetings
- Low response rates from cold outreach
- Sales teams spending time on unqualified prospects
- Lack of a scalable outbound system

This section helps visitors understand the problem being solved.

---

# 5. Solution Overview (Myntmore System)

**Purpose:**

Explain how Myntmore solves the problem.

Suggested structure:

**How Myntmore Works**

Step 1

Identify Ideal Customer Profiles

Step 2

AI-based Prospect Research

Step 3

Personalized LinkedIn Outreach

Step 4

Automated Follow-ups

Step 5

Qualified Meetings Booked

Visual representation of the process is preferred.

---

# 6. Case Studies / Results

**Purpose:**

Demonstrate real outcomes achieved for clients.

Possible elements:

- Case study cards
- Before / after results
- Meeting numbers generated
- Pipeline value created

---

# 7. Founder / Team Section

**Purpose:**

Introduce the founder and build credibility.

Content may include:

- Founder introduction
- Background in lead generation and outbound systems
- Experience working with B2B companies

This section helps humanize the brand.

---

# 8. Service Overview

**Purpose:**

Provide a quick overview of Myntmore's core offerings.

Each service should have a short description.

---

# 9. Strategy Call Section (Primary Conversion)

**Purpose:**

Encourage users to schedule a call.

Description of what the user will get from the call.

Possible bullet points:

- Analysis of current lead generation strategy
- Recommendations for improving outbound performance
- Custom lead generation approach

CTA Button:

**Book Strategy Call**

---

# 10. Calendar Booking Page

Once the user clicks the call booking button, they are redirected to a calendar page.

Form fields may include:

- Name
- Email
- Company name
- Company size
- Target audience
- Current lead generation methods

Purpose:

Qualify leads before the call.

---

# 11. Confirmation Page

After booking the call, the confirmation page should include:

- Confirmation message
- Brief explanation of what will happen during the call

Optional additions:

- Case studies
- Additional resources
- Links to social media
