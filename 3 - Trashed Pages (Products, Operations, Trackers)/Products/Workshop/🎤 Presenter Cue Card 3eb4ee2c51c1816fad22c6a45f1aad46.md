# 🎤 Presenter Cue Card

### AI Hands-On Segment – JBCN Seminar (15–20 min)

---

## 🟦 Activity 1: "AI Already Knows Your School"

`2–3 min` · Whole room · Voice or type

**Say:**

> "Everyone already has an AI assistant on your phone, let's prove it. Open Siri, Google Assistant, or whatever AI app you have, and ask it this exact question."
> 

**Prompt:**

> "Give me one creative icebreaker question for a school parent-teacher night."
> 

**Sample output:**

> "If your child could teach a class on anything for one day, what would it be and why?"
> 

💡 **Tip:** Ask 2–3 people to read their result aloud. Everyone gets a different answer, proving it's generative, not scripted.

---

## 🟦 Activity 2: Personalized Hands-On Prompt

`6–7 min` · Whole room · Text only

**Say:**

> "Now let's make this personal. Open ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude, whichever app you have. Type this, but replace the bracket with something real about your own child or a subject they struggle with."
> 

**Template:**

> "Explain [SUBJECT] to a [AGE]-year-old like a fun bedtime story."
> 

**Sample input:**

> "Explain photosynthesis to a 7-year-old like a fun bedtime story."
> 

**Sample output:**

> "Once upon a time, a little leaf named Leo lived on a big oak tree. Every morning, Leo drank in the air and let the sun give him a hug of light. Using that light, water from his roots, and the air he drank, Leo cooked up his own sugary lunch, and breathed out fresh oxygen for everyone else. That's why Leo and his leafy friends are the real superheroes of the forest..."
> 

**Optional round 2** (if time allows):

**Sample input:**

> "Write a 2-line encouraging note for a student who is nervous about exams."
> 

**Sample output:**

> "You've prepared more than you realize, trust your own hard work. One exam doesn't define you; how you keep showing up does."
> 

💡 **Tip:** Emotional high point. Invite 2–3 parents to read their personalized result aloud.

---

## 🟦 Activity 3: Volunteer Spotlight Demo (On Stage)

`4–5 min`  · Live QR tool

**How it works:**
A volunteer scans a QR code, types their name and their child's interest, and instantly gets a personalized, branded shout-out card displayed on the big screen.

**Sample input:**

> Name: "Ananya" | Child's interest: "robots"
> 

**Sample output (on screen):**

> "Ananya's daughter is building the future one circuit at a time. Today robots, tomorrow the world's most inventive engineer. JBCN is proud to help her get there. 🚀"
> 

💡 **Tip:** Great photo-op moment. Run with a small volunteer group only, not the full room, to avoid connectivity issues.

---

## 🟦 Activity 4: Closer, "Post About It"

`2–3 min` · Media & influencers · Text only

**Say:**

> "Last one, ask your AI app to write a one-line social caption about today, then post it if you'd like."
> 

**Prompt:**

> "Write a short, exciting one-line Instagram caption about attending an AI seminar at my child's school."
> 

**Sample output:**

> "AI just walked into my kid's school, and honestly, the future looks pretty exciting. 🤖📚 #JBCNInnovates"
>
