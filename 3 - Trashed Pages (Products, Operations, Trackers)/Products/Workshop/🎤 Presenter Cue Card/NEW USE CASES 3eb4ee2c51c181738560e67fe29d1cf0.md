# NEW USE CASES

## **👨‍👩‍👧 For Parents & Children**

### Homework & Concept Help

**ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini** – explain any concept at the child's level, step-by-step doubt solving

> *Prompt:* "Explain photosynthesis to a 7-year-old like a fun bedtime story."
> 
> 
> *Prompt:* "My daughter doesn't understand long division. Explain it step by step like I'm teaching a 9-year-old."
> 

**Photomath** – scan a math problem, get step-by-step solving

> *Action:* Point the camera at a printed or handwritten equation (e.g., `2x + 5 = 15`) → app shows each solving step with the final answer, `x = 5`.
> 

**Google Lens** – point camera at a question/diagram, get instant help

> *Action:* Point camera at a labeled diagram of the human heart in a textbook → Lens identifies it and pulls up explanations of each part (atrium, ventricle, valves).
> 

**Khanmigo** (Khan Academy's AI tutor) – Socratic-style tutoring, doesn't just give answers

> *Prompt:* "I'm stuck on this algebra problem: 3x - 7 = 14. Can you help me solve it?"
> 
> 
> *Sample response style:* "Good start! What do you think we should do first to isolate x? Try adding 7 to both sides and tell me what you get."
> 

**Exam prep & flashcards**

- **NotebookLM** – upload a textbook chapter or class notes, auto-generate flashcards, quizzes, and even an audio "podcast" summary of the chapter
- **Quizlet AI** – auto-generates flashcards and practice tests from notes

**Creative & bonding**

- **Claude / ChatGPT voice mode** – speak a story idea out loud, get a full bedtime story back
- **Canva Magic Media** or **Adobe Firefly** – generate images for a school project or story ("draw a dragon who loves math")
- **ElevenLabs** – turn a written story into a narrated audio story in a fun voice

**Practical parenting**

- **ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini** – draft leave applications, emails to teachers
- **Google Read Along** – AI-powered reading practice app for young children

**Accessibility**

- **Voice Access / Google Assistant voice typing** – for kids who struggle with typing
- **Built-in screen readers + AI** – support for children with learning differences

### 📱 For Influencers & Media

**Content creation**

- **CapCut** – AI-powered video editing: auto-captions, jump-cut removal, background music suggestions, all from a phone
- **Descript** – edit video by editing text (delete a word, it cuts that bit from the video); also does AI voiceovers
- **Canva** – quick graphics, templates, and short video content
- **Adobe Firefly / Midjourney** – generate custom images instead of stock photos

**Repurposing & reach**

- **Opus Clip** – turns one long video into multiple short, auto-captioned Reels/Shorts clips
- **ChatGPT / Claude** – turn a long event (like this seminar) into a punchy 3-line caption or reel script
- **ElevenLabs** – AI voiceovers in different tones/languages for reels

**Engagement & workflow**

- **ChatGPT / Claude** – draft consistent, on-brand responses to common DMs/comments
- **Notion AI** – organize and plan a content calendar
- **Claude / ChatGPT + web search** – fact-check a claim before posting something potentially controversial
