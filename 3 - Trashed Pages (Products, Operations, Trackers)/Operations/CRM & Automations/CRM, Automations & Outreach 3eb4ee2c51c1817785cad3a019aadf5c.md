### Cold email rules

- Send only from secondary, warmed-up domains.
- Run 20+ inboxes at no more than 30 emails/day each.
- Keep open tracking off and bounces under 4%.
- Campaign naming: `Client | List | Angle | Domain`.

Domain setup status is in the Domains database ([Domain Tracker](Cold%20Calling%20&%20Cold%20Emailing%20Related/Domain%20Tracker%203eb4ee2c51c18186b1d9f718569735a9.md)).
