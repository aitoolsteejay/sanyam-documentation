Most teams are drowning in tools.

We decided to keep a lean AI stack focused on one thing:

**speed + leverage + operational clarity.**

Here’s the exact AI tool stack we’re moving forward with for meetings, research, documentation, automations, and execution.

### **1. Meeting Intelligence**

### **Fathom**

**Purpose:** AI meeting note taker

**What it handles**

- meeting recordings
- summaries
- action items
- follow-up notes
- searchable conversations

**Why we use it**

Nobody should manually take notes during important calls anymore.

This lets us stay fully present in meetings while still capturing everything automatically.

**Biggest advantage**

Creates accountability and reduces information loss across teams.

### **2. Documentation & Internal Knowledge**

### **Notion + Notion AI**

**Purpose:** Central operating system for the company

**What it handles**

- SOPs
- internal documentation
- meeting notes
- project tracking
- AI-assisted writing
- knowledge management

**Why we use it**

A company without documentation eventually becomes chaos.

Notion AI speeds up writing, organizing, and retrieving information.

**Biggest advantage**

Keeps company knowledge centralized and searchable.

### **3. Core AI Assistant**

### **Anthropic Claude**

**Purpose:** Primary AI reasoning assistant

**What we use it for**

- strategy
- long-form thinking
- workflows
- writing
- system planning
- document analysis

**Why we use it**

Extremely strong for structured thinking and long-context work.

**Biggest advantage**

Handles deep reasoning better than most standard AI workflows.

### **4. AI Research Engine**

### **Perplexity AI**

**Purpose:** Research and intelligence gathering

**What we use it for**

- competitor research
- market analysis
- fast information gathering
- trend monitoring

**Why we use it**

Much faster than traditional searching.

**Biggest advantage**

Condenses research workflows dramatically.

### **5. Workflow Automation**

### **Make**

**Purpose:** Business automation infrastructure

**What it handles**

- automations
- workflows

- notifications
- backend operational tasks

**Why we use it**

Manual repetitive work kills scale.

**Biggest advantage**

Lets small teams operate with massive leverage.

### **6. AI Scheduling**

### **Motion**

**Purpose:** AI-powered scheduling and task planning

**What it handles**

- scheduling
- priority management
- task planning
- focus blocks

**Why we use it**

Helps reduce context switching and scheduling overload.

**Biggest advantage**

Optimizes execution time automatically.

### **7. Calendar Optimization**

### [**Reclaim.ai**](http://Reclaim.ai)

**Purpose:** Smart calendar management

**What it handles**

- calendar optimization
- habit scheduling
- automatic time blocking
- meeting balancing

**Why we use it**

Protects deep work time.

**Biggest advantage**

Creates structure without manual calendar micromanagement.

### **Core Philosophy**

AI should not create more noise.

It should:

- remove repetitive work
- compress execution time
- improve decision making
- reduce operational friction
- increase leverage per employee

The goal is not to use more AI tools.

The goal is to build a faster and more intelligent operating system for the business.
