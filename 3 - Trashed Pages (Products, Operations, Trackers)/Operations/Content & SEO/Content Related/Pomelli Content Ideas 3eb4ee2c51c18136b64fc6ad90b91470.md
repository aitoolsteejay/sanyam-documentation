# 🧠 1. “Pomelli vs Reality” Series (Core pillar)

This should be a **weekly series**.

### Format:

- Show Pomelli output
- Break it down
- Show why it fails / where it works

### Content Ideas:

- “Pomelli writes perfect content. So why doesn’t it convert?”
- “We asked Pomelli to write for founders. It wrote for everyone.”
- “This is why AI content sounds good but feels empty.”

### Twist (important):

Don’t just criticize → **fix it live**

👉 “Here’s how we made this actually convert”
