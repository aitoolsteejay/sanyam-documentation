# Growth Initiatives – MyntMore

---

## 🔍 1. SEO Agent (Top Priority)

**Objective:**

Boost the Domain Authority (DA) and Domain Rating (DR) of [myntmore.com](https://myntmore.com/) through automated and intelligent SEO systems.

**Features:**

- Perform technical SEO audits (on-page, off-page, speed, structure).
- Suggest and auto-generate blog topics based on keyword gaps.
- Automate backlink discovery and outreach recommendations.
- Track competitors’ backlink profiles and content performance.
- Generate monthly strategy reports with action items.

**Why It Matters:**

Higher DA/DR = more organic traffic, stronger credibility, and lower customer acquisition cost over time.

---

## ☎️ 2. Cold Calling Agent

**Objective:**

Streamline and scale outbound cold-calling efforts with smart targeting and personalization.

**Features:**

- Pull and qualify leads from LinkedIn, CSVs, or CRM.
- Generate AI-crafted cold call scripts tailored to industry and ICP.
- Integrate with VoIP systems to log calls and responses.
- Record and analyze call sentiment for agent improvement.
- Auto-schedule follow-up calls based on response likelihood.

**Why It Matters:**

Reduces human effort, improves conversion rates, and ensures consistency in messaging.

---

## ♻️ 3. Content Repurposing Agent

**Objective:**

Turn long-form content into multiple short-form, platform-specific assets.

**Features:**

- Input formats: blog posts, webinars, newsletters, YouTube videos.
- Output formats: LinkedIn carousels, IG captions, tweets, shorts, threads.
- Maintain tone and branding across formats.
- Optimize content based on each platform’s algorithmic preference.
- Auto-schedule or suggest best posting times.

**Why It Matters:**

Maximize ROI on every content piece and maintain consistent brand presence across channels.

---

## 🔥 4. Viral Reel Finder

**Objective:**

Find and recommend trending Reels ideas relevant to MyntMore's niche.

**Features:**

- Monitor IG, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts trends in real-time.
- Filter by geography, hashtags, competitors, and niche.
- Provide weekly trendboards with actionable ideas.
- Suggest scripts, hooks, and visual formats for high-performance content.
- Optionally connect to editor workflows or Canva templates.

**Why It Matters:**

Taps into virality to build awareness, traffic, and social proof organically.

---

## 🗣 5. Feedback Agent

**Objective:**

Collect, analyze, and act on feedback from clients, leads, and team members.

**Features:**

- Auto-send feedback forms after calls, projects, or deals.
- Use NLP to summarize sentiment and extract pain points.
- Create visual dashboards of feedback over time.
- Suggest product/service improvements based on repeated feedback.
- Integrate with Slack for live alerts on negative feedback.

**Why It Matters:**

Data-driven improvement loop that sharpens offerings and enhances client retention.
