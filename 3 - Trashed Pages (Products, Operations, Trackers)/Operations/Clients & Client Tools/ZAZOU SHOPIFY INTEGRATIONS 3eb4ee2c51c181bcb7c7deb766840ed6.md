# ZAZOU SHOPIFY INTEGRATIONS

| **Tool Name** | **Link** | **What It Does (Beginner-Friendly)** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **ReelUp – Shoppable UGC Reels** | [https://apps.shopify.com/reelup](https://apps.shopify.com/reelup) | Adds TikTok/Instagram-style autoplaying video reels on your homepage & product pages. Shows real customers/pets using your products, making the website feel more authentic and increasing trust + conversions. |
| [**Smile.io](http://Smile.io) – Loyalty & Rewards** | [https://apps.shopify.com/smile-io](https://apps.shopify.com/smile-io) | Creates a loyalty program where customers earn points for purchases, referrals, birthdays, etc. Encourages repeat purchases and builds customer loyalty. |
| **Rebuy – Personalization Engine** | [https://apps.shopify.com/rebuy](https://apps.shopify.com/rebuy) | AI-powered product recommendations, personalized upsells, smart cart suggestions, and “complete the look” widgets. Great for boosting Average Order Value (AOV). |
| **Frequently Bought Together (FBT)** | [https://apps.shopify.com/frequently-bought-together](https://apps.shopify.com/frequently-bought-together) | Shows Amazon-style bundles like “Customers also bought…” and lets shoppers add multiple complementary products in one click. Helps increase AOV. |
| **Zoorix Bundles & Cross-Sell** | [https://apps.shopify.com/zoorix](https://apps.shopify.com/zoorix) | Lets you create bundles (ex: Collar + Leash), volume discounts, and cross-sell modules. Also adds smart recommendations on PDPs and cart. |
| **Trust Badges by ShopClimb** | [https://apps.shopify.com/trust-badge](https://apps.shopify.com/trust-badge) | Adds trust badges (ex: Secure Checkout, Money-Back Guarantee, Vet-Approved, Safe Ingredients) to improve credibility and reduce purchase hesitation. |
| **FOMO – Social Proof** | [https://apps.shopify.com/fomo](https://apps.shopify.com/fomo) | Shows real-time notifications like “12 people bought this today” or “Someone from Mumbai purchased this 2 minutes ago.” Creates urgency and increases trust. |
| **Hextom Countdown Timer Bar** | [https://apps.shopify.com/counter](https://apps.shopify.com/counter) | Adds countdown timers and limited-time offer bars (ex: “Sale ends in 02:15:34”). Helps drive urgency and boost conversions. |
| **Vitals – All-In-One Suite** | [https://apps.shopify.com/vitals](https://apps.shopify.com/vitals) | A powerful bundle of 40+ CRO tools (sticky add-to-cart, reviews, volume discounts, countdown timers, trust badges, etc.) in one app. Great value and speeds up the store. |
| **Wheelio / WooHoo Gamified Pop-ups** | Wheelio: [https://apps.shopify.com/wheelio](https://apps.shopify.com/wheelio) | Wheel-style popups that gamify discounts (ex: “Spin to win 10% off”). Increases email capture and makes the experience fun. |
| **EasyTabs / Tabs Studio** | [https://apps.shopify.com/tabs-studio](https://apps.shopify.com/tabs-studio) | Organizes product page content (ingredients, FAQs, safety info, usage instructions) into clean tabs so customers can easily understand product details. |
| [**Judge.me](http://Judge.me) Reviews** | [https://apps.shopify.com/judgeme](https://apps.shopify.com/judgeme) | Collects and displays product reviews, photos, and videos. Supports auto-scroll to reviews, Q&A, and social proof widgets. |
| **Back in Stock: Restock Alerts (Appikon)** | [https://apps.shopify.com/back-in-stock-restock-alerts](https://apps.shopify.com/back-in-stock-restock-alerts) | Lets customers sign up for “Notify me when in stock” alerts via email/SMS. Helps recover lost sales and understand product demand. |
| Chatbot | SmartBot/Tidio | An AI shopping assistant that asks customers what they’re looking for (example: treats, collars, grooming), their pet details (breed, age, size), and their budget. Based on the inputs, the chatbot recommends the best products or bundles and allows customers to instantly add those items to their cart. This improves product discovery, reduces decision fatigue, and increases conversions by giving personalized, guided shopping. |
