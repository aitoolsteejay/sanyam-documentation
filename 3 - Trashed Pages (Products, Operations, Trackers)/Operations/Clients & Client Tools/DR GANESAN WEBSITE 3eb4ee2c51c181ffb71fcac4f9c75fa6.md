1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

We will be undertaking an end-to-end revamp of the KMS Pharma website, along with a comprehensive SEO program covering On-Page, Off-Page, AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation), and GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) — both as a one-time setup and as an ongoing (recurring) engagement.

---

1. SCOPE OF WORK

A. Website Revamp (Design, UX & Content)

- Revamp of the Home Page
- Revamp of all Service Pages
- End-to-end content rewrite/creation for all pages
- AI-assisted UI/UX design and development

B. Initial Strategy

- Sitemap planning and restructuring
- In-depth competitor analysis – top 8–10 competitors (positioning, keywords, backlink profile, content gaps)
- Content and SEO strategy roadmap

C. One-Time SEO

1. Keyword Research – 100+ target keywords mapped to relevant pages
2. On-Page SEO – optimisation across up to 20 pages (meta tags, headers, content, internal linking, schema)
3. Off-Page SEO – 20–25 quality backlinks/citations built
4. AEO – FAQ schema and structured data implementation across up to 10 key pages
5. GEO – content optimisation for AI search/generative engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) across up to 10 key pages
6. Competitor Analysis – detailed benchmarking of top 8–10 competitors

D. Lead Magnet Creation (One-Time, included in this phase)
As part of the strategy, we will design and build the following lead magnets to help convert website visitors into qualified leads:

1. Dossier Development Readiness Checklist – a downloadable self-assessment tool that helps prospective pharma/biopharma clients evaluate whether their product is ready for dossier submission (covering technical, BA/BE and regulatory gaps). This positions KMS as the expert partner who can close those gaps.
2. "Reducing Time-to-Market" White paper – a flagship white paper highlighting how KMS Pharma's integrated in-house infrastructure (formulation development, BA/BE, clinical research, dossier development and regulatory support) compresses typical development-to-launch timelines compared to a conventional multi-vendor model. This is designed to be KMS's strongest differentiator-led asset for attracting virtual/biopharma companies and international partners.

Both assets will be gated behind a simple contact form to capture qualified leads.
