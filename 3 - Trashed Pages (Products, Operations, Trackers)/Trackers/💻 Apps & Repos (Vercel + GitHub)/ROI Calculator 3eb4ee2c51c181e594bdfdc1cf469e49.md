What it is: Single-page outreach ROI calculator used on sales calls: funnel (requests, accepted, replies, meetings, deals) to revenue, Full vs Effective LTV, ROI %, revenue/cost chart, 9 currencies (INR lakh formatting), PDF export. Vite + React 18 + shadcn/ui, first scaffolded in Lovable. No backend, no lead gate, no env vars. Quietly adds 1 percentage point to the closing rate (shown as 'Adjusted Rate').
