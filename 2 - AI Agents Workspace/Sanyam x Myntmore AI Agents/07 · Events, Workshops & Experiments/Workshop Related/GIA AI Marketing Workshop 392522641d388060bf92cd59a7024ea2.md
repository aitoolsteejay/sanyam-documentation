## 01 - Tell Us About Your GIA Business

Share the context that shapes everything else in this workshop.

**What to cover:**

- Your specialty: retail, estate jewelry, lab grading, appraisals, or wholesale
- Who your clients are: collectors, designers, jewelers, brides, insurers
- Your revenue stage: just starting, growing, scaling
- Where you want to grow: new clients, new markets, more visibility

**Your profile tags:**`Gemologist` `Appraiser` `Retailer` `Lab Professional`

## 02 - Optimize Your Online Presence

Your GIA credentials deserve a strong first impression wherever clients find you. Work through whichever channels apply to you - you can have more than one.

> 💡 **No presence yet?** Skip ahead to Step 06 - that's where your online presence gets built from scratch. Everything here will still connect.
> 

### LinkedIn *(if applicable)*

Rewrite your headline and About section to lead with your GIA credentials (GG, AJP, etc.) and attract your ideal clients - not just connections in the trade.

- Headline formula: `[GIA Credential] helping [ICP] [achieve outcome]`
- About section: open with the client's problem, not your résumé
- Featured section: link to your lead magnet (built in Step 04)

### Instagram *(if applicable)*

- Bio: mention your GIA cert, specialty, and location in the first line
- Define 3 content pillars (e.g. gem education, grading walkthroughs, behind-the-scenes)
- Link-in-bio: point to a lead magnet or booking page, not your homepage

### Website / Google *(if applicable)*

- Homepage headline: lead with GIA authority + what you do + who you serve
- Google Business Profile: add GIA credentials, specialty, service area, and photos
- SEO: target searches like "GIA certified gemologist [city]" or "diamond appraisal [city]"

### None Yet

No problem - Step 06 is where you walk away with a ready-to-use website prompt and outreach playbook to build from the ground up.

## 03 - Define Your Gem Niche ICP & Value Prop

Get razor-sharp on who you actually serve and what makes your GIA expertise the clear choice.

**Ideal Client Profile (ICP) questions to answer:**

- Who are they? *(high-end retail buyer, estate dealer, jewelry designer, insurance appraiser)*
- What are they searching for? *(trust, certified expertise, specific gem category)*
- What problem do they have that your GIA credential solves?
- Where do they hang out? *(Instagram, referrals, trade shows, Google search)*

**Value Prop framework:**

> *"I help [ICP] who [problem/desire] get [outcome] through [your GIA-backed method]."*
> 

**Example:**

> *"I help jewelry designers source ethically certified colored stones with confidence, using GIA grading to eliminate guesswork and protect their reputation."*
> 

**Niche tags to pick from:**`Colored Stones` `Diamonds` `Estate & Antique` `Engagement Rings` `Appraisals` `Lab-Grown`

## 04 - Build Your GTM & Gem Education Lead Magnets

Map your go-to-market approach and create content your ICP is already searching for.

**Go-To-Market approach:**

## 05 - Create AI-Generated Product Visuals

Use AI image generators to create stunning model shots, lifestyle imagery, and product displays - no photoshoot budget needed. Use these visuals across your social content, lead magnets, and website.

### Tool

| Tool | Best For |
| --- | --- |
| **Gemini (Google)** | Photorealistic model shots - rings on hands, necklaces on necks, earrings on models |

### Prompt Template for Jewelry Model Shots (Gemini)

```
A close-up, photorealistic image of a [ring/necklace/earrings] featuring a [gem type, e.g. oval sapphire] set in [metal, e.g. 18k white gold], worn by a [model description, e.g. woman with elegant hands], on a [background, e.g. soft cream linen background], soft natural lighting, luxury editorial style, high resolution
```

**Tips:**

- Always specify lighting: *soft natural*, *golden hour*, *studio white*
- Mention the setting/background to control the mood
- Add "luxury editorial," "GIA certified," or "fine jewelry campaign" to push quality
- Use Firefly for background swaps if you have real product photos

## 06 - Generate Your Website Copy & Outreach Playbook

Walk away with everything you need to show up, attract, and convert your ideal clients.

### Website Prompt Template

Use this as your AI prompt to generate your homepage copy:

```
Write homepage copy for a GIA-certified [specialty, e.g. gemologist and appraiser] based in [city].
My ideal client is [ICP from Step 03]. My value prop is [value prop from Step 03].
I want the tone to be [e.g. warm and authoritative / clean and minimal / educational and trustworthy].
Include: a headline, subheadline, 3 service sections, a trust section highlighting my GIA credentials, and a CTA.
```

### Outreach Playbook by ICP

**For retail jewelry buyers:**

> Lead with education, not a pitch. Share a grading report explainer or buying guide. Offer a free 15-min consult to answer gem questions.
> 

**For jewelry designers:**

> Focus on sourcing confidence and certification. Show you can help them communicate quality to their end clients.
> 

**For estate clients / insurers:**

> Lead with credentials and process. Emphasize GIA's global authority and your appraisal methodology.
> 

**For cold outreach - message framework:**

1. Reference something specific *(their collection, a piece they posted, a shared connection)*
2. Establish GIA credibility in one line
3. Offer a specific value *(a guide, a quick answer, a resource)*
4. One clear CTA *(reply, DM, book a call)*

> 🔗 If you skipped Step 02, use the website prompt above to build your online presence from scratch before starting outreach.
