# Posting Rhythm Builder

Link: https://posting-rhythm-builder.vercel.app/
Marketing Strategy: A. Relatable founder posts“Most founders don’t fail at content because of quality.

They fail because their calendar is unrealistic.”
Then:“We built a tool that creates a posting rhythm based on your actual life.”
This feels human, not salesy.

B. Carousel idea (very strong)
Slide 1: “Why founders stop posting on LinkedIn”

Slide 2: Too busy

Slide 3: No ideas

Slide 4: Unrealistic calendars

Slide 5: Solution → posting rhythm, not volume
CTA:“Build a posting rhythm you won’t quit.”
Use of the tool: This tool creates a personalized LinkedIn posting schedule based on your lifestyle, ICP behavior, and content strengths.
It tells you exactly when to post and what to post so you stay consistent without burnout.
