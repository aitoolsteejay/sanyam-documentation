# ICP Builder & Value Proposition Generator - Non Technical Documentation

### **What it does**

The ICP Builder & Value Proposition Generator is a free AI tool that helps a business owner figure out exactly who to sell to and exactly what to say to them. Instead of vague labels like "mid-market SaaS companies," it produces detailed customer profiles ("Ideal Customer Profiles," or ICPs) that describe a real type of buyer: their role or lifestyle, their day-to-day pressures, what makes them buy, what objections they raise, where they spend their time online, and how to talk to them. It then takes each of those profiles and writes a matching value proposition: a positioning statement, a before/after story, a 3-step "how it works" pitch, and a short elevator pitch, all personalized to that specific customer type.

It's built for founders, marketers, and salespeople who are tired of generic personas that could describe any company in their industry. The tool explicitly distinguishes between B2B buyers (professionals buying on behalf of an organization) and D2C consumers (individuals making a personal purchase), and adapts everything — tone, channels, psychology — depending on which one it's building for. It also identifies "channel partners" for each profile: real types of businesses, consultants, or influencers who already have that customer's trust and could refer or co-sell on the business's behalf, and generates a separate value proposition aimed at pitching those partners.

This is the largest and most complex of the company's nine free tools. Where the other tools are single-shot generators, this one runs a guided, multi-step wizard: describe the business, build up to six customer profiles, generate the ICPs, then generate value props for each one (plus channel partners) in a second AI pass. It exists to give prospective customers (leads) a genuinely useful, done-for-them strategy artifact in exchange for their contact details, while showcasing the depth of the company's strategic thinking.

### **How someone uses it, step by step**

1. **Landing page**: The visitor first sees a marketing/explainer page (`LandingPage.tsx`) with a hero ("Stop Guessing Who Your Customer Is"), problem/solution sections, a 3-step "How It Works" explainer, and two "Build My ICP Now" calls to action.
2. **Lead gate**: Clicking through triggers a lead-capture form (shared `LeadGate` component) asking for First Name, Last Name, Designation (all required), plus optional Company, Phone, LinkedIn URL, and Email. Submitting is required before any AI generation happens.
3. **Step 1 — Intake screen** (`IntakeScreen.tsx`): The user types a free-text description of their "Core Offer." After they stop typing for 900ms, the tool auto-generates 3 polished rewritten versions of the offer and the user must pick (and can edit) one. They also pick "Selling To" (D2C / B2B / Both) and "Business Type" (Product-based / Service-based / Hybrid). All three fields are required to continue.
4. **Step 2 — ICP Builder screen** (`IcpBuilderScreen.tsx`): Starts with 3 ICP cards (`DEFAULT_ICP_COUNT`), expandable up to 6 (`MAX_ICP_COUNT`) only if "Selling To" was "Both." For each ICP marked B2B, the user selects Industries (max 3), Target Geography, Roles, and Company Size via a searchable `MultiSelect` component (with an "Other" free-text option). If the geography includes India, an extra "Cities in India" text field appears. For a D2C-marked ICP, the user instead free-types a customer description, which similarly gets auto-polished into 3 selectable/editable versions. Once all cards are valid, the user clicks "Generate ICP Profiles," which calls the AI and displays results in `IcpResultsTabs` (one tab per ICP, plus a "Channel Partners" tab if any were generated), covering: Who They Are, Core Responsibilities/Daily Life, Psychology, Pain Points, Goals & Desires, Buying Triggers, Objections, How to Position, Where They Hang Out, Geography Context, and Channel Partners. Users can regenerate or continue to Step 3.
5. **Step 3 — Value Proposition screen** (`ValuePropScreen.tsx`): Clicking "Generate Value Proposition" sends the ICP results to a second AI call and returns one tab per ICP (plus a Channel Partners tab, if applicable). Each ICP tab shows: Core Promise, Before/After state, a 3-Step System, Why Others Fail, Why You Win, Content Strategy, Short Pitch, Call to Action, Core Angle (Authority/ROI/Speed/Trust with explanation), and a Core Positioning Statement. The Channel Partners tab instead shows What's In It For Them, Ideal Partner Profile, How the Partnership Works (3 steps), Why Partner With Us, How to Approach Them, Short Pitch, CTA, Positioning, and Core Angle. Every text block has a `CopyButton` for one-click copying. Clicking "Done" scrolls back to the top.

Throughout, a 3-step progress indicator (Intake → ICP Builder → Value Proposition) is shown at the top of the page.
