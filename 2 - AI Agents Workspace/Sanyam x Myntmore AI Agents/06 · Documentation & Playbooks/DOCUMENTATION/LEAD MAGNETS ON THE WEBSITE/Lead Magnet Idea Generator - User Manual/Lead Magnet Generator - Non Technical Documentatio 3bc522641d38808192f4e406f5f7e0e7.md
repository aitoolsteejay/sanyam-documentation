### **What it does**

The Lead Magnet Idea Generator is a free tool on Myntmore's site that helps B2B founders figure out what "lead magnet" (a piece of content or a small free tool used to earn a reply in cold email or LinkedIn outreach) they should actually build. A lot of founders know they need one but get stuck at "write an ebook" or "make a checklist," which is too vague to act on and rarely converts. This tool solves that by asking a few questions about the business and spitting back three concrete, tailored ideas.

It exists as both a genuinely useful free tool and a lead-generation funnel for Myntmore itself: anyone who wants to use it has to first fill out a contact form (name, company, designation, email, phone, LinkedIn), which is captured as a sales lead before they ever see their results. After showing the three ideas, the page ends with a pitch to book a call with Myntmore, since Myntmore's actual business is building and running these kinds of systems for clients.

The value delivered to the visitor: instead of a generic idea, they get three lead magnet concepts (calculators, benchmark reports, audits, templates, checklists, swipe files, short assessments — explicitly not generic ebooks) that are grounded in their specific business description, target audience (ICP), industry, and preferred tone, each with a written pitch, an explanation of why it should work psychologically, and a specific instruction on where/when to use it in an outreach sequence.

### **How someone uses it, step by step**

1. **Landing page** (`/tools/lead-magnet-ideas`): a marketing page (hero, "your lead magnet ideas aren't landing" problem section, "how it works" steps, value props, and two CTA buttons — "Generate My Ideas" / "Generate My Ideas Now") explaining the tool and inviting the visitor to start.
2. **Lead gate**: clicking start reveals the shared `LeadGate` form ("Tell Us About You" / "Enter your details to unlock your custom lead magnet ideas"). The visitor must supply First Name, Last Name, and Designation (required); Company, Phone, LinkedIn URL, and Email are optional. Submitting this form is what unlocks the actual input step.
3. **Input panel**: once past the gate, the visitor fills in:
    - "What does your business do?" (free-text business description)
    - "Target Audience / ICP" (free-text)
    - "Industry" (dropdown of preset options, or "Other" with a custom text field)
    - "Preferred Tone" (one of: Authoritative, Professional & Warm, Sharp & Direct, Friendly, Visionary) Then clicks "Generate Ideas."
4. **Results**: after a loading spinner, three idea cards appear, each showing a title, a pitch, a "Why It Works" explanation, and a "How to Use It" (distribution) note, with a per-card "Copy" button that copies the idea as plain text to the clipboard. The page scrolls to top and ends with a "We can build and run these for you" call-to-action linking to `/founder-meeting` to book a call.
