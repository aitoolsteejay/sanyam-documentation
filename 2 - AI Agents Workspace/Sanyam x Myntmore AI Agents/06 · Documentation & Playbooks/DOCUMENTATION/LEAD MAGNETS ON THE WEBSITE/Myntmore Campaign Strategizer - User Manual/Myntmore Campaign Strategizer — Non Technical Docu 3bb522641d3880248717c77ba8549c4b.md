## What is this tool?

This is an internal tool that writes LinkedIn outreach messages for you. You describe a client, who they're trying to reach, and how they want to sound — and the tool uses AI to generate a full outreach campaign: a connection request note plus a series of follow-up messages, each written in three different versions to choose from.

---

## What it actually produces

For every campaign, you get:

- **A strategy summary** — explaining the thinking behind the messaging approach
- **3 versions of a connection note** — the short message sent with a LinkedIn connection request
- **1 to 3 follow-up messages** (your choice) — each also with 3 versions
- Warnings if a message is too long or too short
- The ability to regenerate just one section, change the tone, or revise based on your own notes
- A one-click "copy" button for grabbing any message

## How to use it, step by step

**1. Pick or add a client**
You can select a previously saved client (which auto-fills their name, company, and what they sell), or add a brand-new one. You can also edit an existing saved client's details.

**2. Describe what the client sells — focus on the outcome, not just the product**
Instead of "they sell air purifiers," the tool wants something like: "they provide measurable indoor air protection that reduces health complaints and improves the workplace." The more outcome-focused the description, the better the AI's output will be.

**3. Fill in strategy details**

- Which industries to target
- The main problem the campaign should focus on
- A "narrative angle" — e.g. should the client come across as an authority, a contrarian, an ROI-focused expert, etc. (you can pick more than one)
- The tone — analytical, bold, curious, direct, and so on (pick just one)

**4. Describe who you're reaching (the audience)**

- Job titles (Founder, CEO, Head of HR, etc.)
- Their industry
- Their pain points — choose from a list, or type your own

**5. Set up the campaign structure**

- How many follow-up messages (1–3)
- What free offer to mention (a free tool, audit, or consultation call)
- What kind of call-to-action to use (soft, direct, or "offer first")

**6. Add any hard rules**
You can type instructions like "don't mention audits" or "keep sentences short" and the AI will follow them.

**7. Click "Generate Campaign"**
The AI creates the full campaign in a few seconds.

**8. Review, tweak, and copy**
You can switch between the three versions of any message, regenerate just one section you don't like, ask the AI to make it more strategic/curious/insight-led, or type your own revision notes and have the whole thing rewritten while keeping the original structure. When you're happy, click "Copy Message" to grab the text.

## Built-in rules the AI always follows

- Connection notes stay under 300 characters (LinkedIn's practical limit).
- Follow-up messages are at least 450 characters, so they don't feel thin.
- Every follow-up ends with a call-to-action.
- The tool actively avoids generic, overused phrasing.
- It never uses the word "help" (or "helping," "helped," "helpful") — this is intentionally filtered out, both by instructing the AI and by double-checking the output afterward.

## Things worth knowing (in case something looks off)

1. **Saved clients only live in your own browser right now.** They're not shared across different computers, browsers, or team members yet — meaning if a teammate adds a client on their laptop, you won't see it on yours. A shared, synced version of this feature is planned but not built yet.
2. **Clearing your browser's data will wipe your saved clients.** If browser history/storage gets cleared, or you switch to a different browser or incognito mode, custom clients you've added will disappear (unless they were also one of the built-in defaults).
3. **Generated campaigns aren't saved anywhere yet.** There's currently no history — once you leave the page, that specific campaign output is gone unless you copied the parts you wanted.
4. **You can't directly edit the generated text inside the tool yet.** If you want to tweak wording slightly, you'd need to copy it out and edit it elsewhere, or ask the AI to regenerate that section with new instructions.
5. **There's no login system yet.** Anyone with access to the tool can use it and see/add saved clients — there's no separate "who's allowed to do what" system in place at this stage.

## What's planned next

According to the internal roadmap, upcoming improvements include:

- Making saved clients sync across the whole team (instead of being stuck in one browser)
- Saving a history of past campaigns
- Letting people edit the generated messages directly, with a version history
- An automatic quality checker (flagging repetition, banned words, tone mismatches, etc.)
- A team workflow with stages like draft → review → approved → launched
- Importing lists of prospects and tracking reply/meeting results over time

## Quick answers to common questions

- **"Will the same client I saved show up if I open this on another computer?"** Not currently — saved clients are tied to the specific browser they were added in.
- **"What happens if a generated message is too short or too long?"** The tool will show a warning, but it still lets you copy and use the message — you can also just regenerate that section for a better length.
- **"Can I trust the AI's output to send as-is?"** It's designed to follow your rules and tone closely, but it's still good practice to give generated copy a quick read before sending it to a real prospect.
