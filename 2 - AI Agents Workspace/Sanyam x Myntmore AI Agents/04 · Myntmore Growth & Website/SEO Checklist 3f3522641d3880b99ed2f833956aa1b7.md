## SEO checklist expansion — October 2026

The four cadence subpages now contain **72 scheduled tasks**: **15 weekly, 11 bi-weekly, 27 monthly and 19 quarterly**, including 13 new tasks focused on attribution, conversion, lead-form reliability, AI visibility, local SEO, SERP intent, evidence and crawler access. Existing 59 tasks and their Codex/Claude prompts remain in place.

### Existing-task refinements (guidance, not extra tasks)

- **M7 llms.txt:** Keep link hygiene as an optional documentation/discoverability check, not a proven ranking factor. Reduce priority below canonical/indexing and conversion issues.
- **M19 FAQ backfill:** Add FAQs only when genuinely useful for the page and user intent; do not assume FAQPage markup yields rich results on an agency site. Treat 29/29 FAQ schema coverage as a review target, not a mandatory SEO outcome.
- **Q12 thin posts:** Prioritize content usefulness, missing information, evidence and intent. Word count is a diagnostic, not a ranking target; do not pad content solely to hit 800 words.
- **M11/Q4 backlinks:** Investigate suspicious links before considering disavow; do not automatically disavow domains based on industry labels.
- **M13 AI visibility:** Keep prompt tests as directional observations with model/date and repeated runs, not definitive rankings.

### Implementation order

1. First: W14 organic-to-CRM attribution, W15 lead-form reliability and M21 conversion funnels.
2. Next: B10 prioritization, B11 experiment log, M24 keyword gaps and M25 search intent.
3. Then: M22 AI visibility, M23 Google Business Profile, Q18 evidence review and Q19 crawler access.

**Additional event-based check:** Before each production deployment, run automated SEO regression tests on changed routes (metadata, canonical, robots/noindex, redirects, sitemap, links and JSON-LD). This is a release gate rather than a calendar task, so it is not counted among the 72 scheduled tasks. Do not deploy without existing approvals.

**Measurement:** Compare actual human audit minutes and remediation minutes across two cycles before recalibrating effort and automation-savings estimates.
