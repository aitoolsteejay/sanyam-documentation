# Information on DA & DR

### ⚙️ What DA and DR Mean:

- **DA (Domain Authority)** – Moz metric (1–100) estimating how well your domain will rank on Google. Focuses on **quality + quantity of backlinks**.
- **DR (Domain Rating)** – Ahrefs metric (1–100) showing the **strength of your backlink profile**.

---

### 📊 Current Scores:

- **DA: 5** → Very low — Moz sees few or weak backlinks.
- **DR: 46** → Above average — Ahrefs sees good backlink strength.

This mismatch is common. Moz may not have indexed your strongest links yet.

---

### 🧠 What To Do To Increase DA & DR:

### 1. **Get Good Websites to Link to You**

Links from other websites to yours = votes of trust

To get more of these:

- **Write blog posts** for popular sites in your industry.
- **Answer journalist questions** on [HARO](https://www.helpareporter.com/) to get featured (and linked).
- **Add your site** to directories or forums related to your topic.
- **Give real value** — don't just trade links or spam people.

### 2. **Make Sure Your Site Works Well for Google**

- Google should be able to **read** (crawl) your site easily.
- Use proper **titles, headings, and links** within your content.
- Make sure your site **loads fast** and works on **phones** too.
