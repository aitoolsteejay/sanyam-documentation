## 🔹 Step 3: Pricing Engine (Data-Based Calculation)

Once similar items are found, the system calculates pricing in a structured way.

### Step-by-step logic:

1. **Base Price**
    - Average of similar past items
2. **Material Adjustment**
    - Fabric vs laminate vs acrylic
    - Premium materials increase cost
3. **Size Adjustment**
    - Larger dimensions → higher pricing
4. **Quantity Impact**
    - Bulk orders may reduce per-unit cost
5. **Standard Cost Additions**
    - Fabric per meter
    - Finishing
    - Hardware assumptions

---

### 📤 Output Generated:

- Price Range
- Recommended Price
- Cost Breakdown

---

### Example:

Input: "Fabric sofa"

Output:

- ₹15,000 – ₹40,000
- Recommended: ₹22,000
- Based on past BOQs
