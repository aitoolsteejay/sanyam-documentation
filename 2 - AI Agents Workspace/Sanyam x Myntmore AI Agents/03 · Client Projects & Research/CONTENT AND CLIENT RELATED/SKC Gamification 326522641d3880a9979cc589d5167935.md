# SKC Gamification

# 🎮 1. Scale-Up Readiness Simulator

👉 *“What’s broken in my company right now?”*

---

## 🧠 What this tool REALLY does

It takes messy business problems and tells you:

- Where you are (Burnout / Survival / SSJ)
- What exactly is missing
- What single thing you should fix first

---

## 🎮 Gamification (simple version)

- You have a **Growth Meter**
- Some parts are “complete”
- Some parts are “open loops” (unfinished systems)

👉 Your job = **close the loops**

---

## 🔍 Think of it like:

A game showing:

> “⚠️ Culture = incomplete”
> 
> 
> “⚠️ Vision alignment = weak”
> 
> “✅ Revenue growth = strong”
> 

---

## 💬 Dummy Example (simplified)

### Input:

> “Revenue is growing fast, but my team keeps quitting and everything feels chaotic.”
> 

---

### Output:

**Your State:** Burnout Zone

(You’re growing fast but internally unstable)

**What’s broken:**

- Team doesn’t feel connected to vision
- No structure → everything is reactive

**Open Loop Identified:**

👉 “Culture Alignment”

**Next Move:**

> Sit with your team and define:
> 
> - Why your company exists
> - What kind of culture you want

---

## 🧠 In one line:

👉 This tool = **Problem Detector**

---

# 🎮 2. Legacy Architect Roadmap

👉 *“Can my company survive without me?”*

---

## 🧠 What this tool REALLY does

It checks:

> “If you disappear for 30 days… does your company collapse?”
> 

---

## 🎮 Gamification

- You have a **Legacy Score**
- Low score = company depends on you
- High score = company runs without you

👉 Goal = **become replaceable (as a system, not as a person)**

---

## 🔍 Think of it like:

Right now:

> You = bottleneck
> 

This tool helps you become:

> System = driver
> 

---

## 💬 Dummy Example (simplified)

### Input:

> “My team is good, but every important decision still comes to me.”
> 

---

### Output:

**Your Status:** Growing, but not scalable

**Big Risk:**

👉 You are the system

(not good for scaling)

**Broken Pillar:**

👉 “Delegation & Succession”

---

### 🎯 Mission:

**“The Legacy Architect”**

**Your Task:**

- Pick 3 decisions you currently make
- Assign them to team leads
- Let them own it fully

---

### 🎁 Reward:

- You free up time
- Company becomes scalable

---

## 🧠 In one line:

👉 This tool = **Dependency Killer**

---

# 🎮 3. Culture & Purpose Pulse-Check

👉 *“Does my team actually care… or just work for salary?”*

---

## 🧠 What this tool REALLY does

It checks:

- Do people believe in your vision?
- Or are they just doing a job?

---

## 🎮 Gamification

- You have a **Purpose Score**
- Low = people working for money
- High = people working for mission

👉 Goal = **people WANT to build, not HAVE to**

---

## 🔍 Think of it like:

Two companies:

**Company A:**

- People wait for salary
- No emotional connection

**Company B:**

- People feel “this is mine”
- They care deeply

👉 You want B

---

## 💬 Dummy Example (simplified)

### Input:

> “We have a vision written, but no one really cares. People only focus on bonuses.”
> 

---

### Output:

**Your State:** Success without Joy

(You’re making money, but culture is weak)
